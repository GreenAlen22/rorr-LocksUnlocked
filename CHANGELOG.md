# Changelog

All notable LocksUnlocked changes.

## [1.1.6]

### Added

- Pick lock: a second prompt at every chest, F by default. Beat the minigame and the chest opens for free.
- 9 lockpicking minigames: Pins, Ring, Lockpick, Pressure, Cipher, Circuit, Probe, Frequency and Code.
- 4 lock levels, from the small Chest to the Legendary Chest, each with its own time limit.
- A clean pick can roll an item from a better chest, and a streak of picks gives up to 30% chance at a double drop.
- Mistakes raise the chest's price and make noise. The third one breaks the lock and takes 15% of your gold.
- Enemies lose track of you while you pick.
- Robomando's Re-wire starts the minigame too, one level easier and without noise or cost. Only he can pick drones and other paid machines.
- Multiplayer, English and Russian.
